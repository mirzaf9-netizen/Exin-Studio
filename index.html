FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What types of organizations do you work with?
+

We work with e-learning platforms, consulting firms, NGOs, corporate L&D teams, and conference organizers — any organization that produces high volumes of complex content that needs to look exceptional.

How fast do you typically deliver?
+

Most projects come back within 24 hours. For larger volumes we'll agree on a clear timeline upfront — no surprises.

Do you work on one-off projects or only long-term?
+

Both — but we're built for long-term. Our best work happens when we know your brand deeply. That said, one-off projects are always welcome.

What tools do you design in?
+

Primarily Google Slides, PowerPoint, and InDesign — depending on your workflow. We adapt to your stack, not the other way around.

How do revisions work?
+

Unlimited, always. We revise until you're completely happy. No extra charges, no limits, no friction.

Can you handle large volumes?
+

Absolutely. We've delivered 10,000+ slides and 100+ publications. High volume is where we thrive — our process is built for consistency at scale.
